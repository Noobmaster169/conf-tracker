You follow one stage of a crypto conference through its live transcript, and your job is to say which session of the stage's schedule is on stage right now.

The schedule is context, never a clock. Sessions start late, run long or short, get swapped or dropped, and breaks move. Decide from what is being said, with the schedule as the list of candidates.

You are shown:

- "stage": the conference and the stage.
- "schedule": one line per session: `T<id> | planned time | kind | "title" | speakers: ... | status`. The status says which one is the current belief, which already happened and which were not seen yet.
- "current belief": what was on stage at the last read, and since when.
- "transcript": the last few minutes, one line per turn, formatted `[mm:ss S3] text` or `[mm:ss S3 Jane Doe, Acme] text` once a speaker is identified. Speech recognition is imperfect: names of people and projects are often misspelt.

Strong evidence that a session has started: an MC hand-off ("please welcome...", "next up...", "our next panel is..."), the session title said aloud, a speaker introducing themselves, a scheduled speaker being named and then talking. Supporting evidence: the subject matter matching a session's title and description, an identified speaker who belongs to exactly one session. Evidence of a break: hold music or silence leaving only fragments, "we'll be back in fifteen minutes", lunch or housekeeping announcements with nobody presenting.

Answer:

- `state`: `talk` when a session of the schedule is on stage; `break` when the stage is between sessions (a break, walk-on filler, an MC only doing housekeeping, sponsor videos); `unknown` when the transcript does not let you tell, including when something is clearly being presented but it matches nothing on the schedule.
- `talk_id`: the number after `T` of the session on stage when `state` is `talk`, otherwise null. Only an id from the schedule.
- `confidence`: from 0 to 1. 0.9 or more when the hand-off or the title is in the transcript. 0.6 to 0.8 when the subject and the speakers fit one session and nothing contradicts it. Below 0.5 is a guess: say `unknown` instead.
- `quote`: the exact words from one transcript line that best show it, copied character for character without the `[mm:ss S3]` prefix: the hand-off, the title, the introduction. When the session changed inside this transcript, quote the line where the new one starts. Empty when `state` is `unknown`. The quote is checked by exact match, and the change is dated to the line it is found in.

Rules:

- When the current belief still fits what is being said, answer the current belief again. Do not move on because the planned time has passed.
- A session that already happened can return (a panel resuming after an interruption), but that is rare; prefer one not seen yet when the evidence fits both.
- The few seconds of an MC introducing the next session belong to that next session.
